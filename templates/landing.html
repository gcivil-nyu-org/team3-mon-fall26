Questions.

Is NYC Active free?

Yes. Creating an account, joining events and organizing your own are all free.

Who can organize an event?

Any signed-in member. When you create an event you become its organizer, so you can edit the details, set the capacity and manage who's going.

What happens when an event fills up?

New RSVPs are blocked once an event reaches its capacity. If someone cancels, their spot opens up again.

Where does the park information come from?

Park details come from NYC Open Data, the city's public data platform.
