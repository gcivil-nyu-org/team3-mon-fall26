# NYC Active: Park Explorer & Recreation Meetup

NYC Active is a community platform for discovering NYC parks and recreational facilities, organizing local sports meetups, and sharing useful information about recreational spaces.

Built by Team 3 for NYU's Fall 2026 Monday Software Engineering class.

## Why NYC Active?

Information about parks and athletic amenities is spread across different sites. Finding a suitable court or running track, finding people to exercise with, and coordinating a meetup often require separate tools. NYC Active brings park discovery, recreational events, and community feedback together.

## Who is it for?

- **Recreational participants:** NYC residents, students, and visitors looking for places and people to exercise or play sports with.
- **Event organizers:** Users who create meetups, manage attendees, and coordinate event logistics.
- **Administrators:** Users responsible for park information, reports, content moderation, and account administration.

## Planned MVP

- **Park and facility discovery:** An interactive map and list, park details, and filters for borough or neighborhood and athletic amenities.
- **Accounts and profiles:** Sign-up and login, sports preferences, casual or competitive skill level, and home borough.
- **Recreational events:** Create, browse, edit, and cancel public meetups attached to park locations.
- **Event participation:** Join or leave events, enforce optional participant limits, and display attendee rosters.
- **Event discussions:** Coordinate logistics through event-specific threads and organizer announcements.
- **Park reviews:** Share text reviews and 1–5 star ratings about amenities and facility conditions.
- **Administration:** Maintain park information, handle reports, moderate content, and manage user status.

## Future features

These features are outside the initial MVP:

- Expiring court check-ins and crowdsourced busy meters.
- Event waitlists with automatic promotion.
- Live borough broadcasts for games looking for players.
- Attendee reliability scores and sportsmanship badges.
- Live facility-condition reporting, event recommendations, weather integration, and calendar syncing.

Exact dataset links, field mappings, attribution requirements, and update procedures will be documented once the team selects the datasets. Administrators will be able to add missing facilities and correct park information.
